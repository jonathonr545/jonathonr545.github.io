Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Job Agent is a personal tool with one user: its owner, Jonathon Ruzicka.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, Job Agent reads the owner's own Gmail with read-only access, the gmail.readonly scope. It looks for job alert emails and replies from employers. It cannot send, change or delete email.

Where the data goes

What it reads stays on the owner's laptop. To score job listings, the text of job-related emails may be sent to the Anthropic API for processing. Sign-in tokens are kept in Windows Credential Manager. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Google user data

Job Agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page, under Security, in the list of third-party apps with account access.
